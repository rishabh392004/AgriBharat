Questions farmers ask

Who can use Krishi Darphan?

Any farmer, gardener or agri student. Create a free account and you can start scanning right away.

Is it really free?

Yes. Scanning crops and reading treatment tips costs nothing.

What should I photograph?

One affected leaf, filling most of the frame, in good daylight. Avoid shadows and keep the leaf in focus.

How accurate is the diagnosis?

The result shows a likely disease with high confidence. For severe outbreaks, confirm with your local agriculture officer.

Which crops are supported?

16 major Indian crops including Wheat, Rice, Tomato, Potato, Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean, and Mustard.
